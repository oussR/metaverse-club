Who are we?

The Metaverse Club is a scientific and creative club at our university. We bring together students who are passionate about creativity, innovation and modern digital technologies.

Our mission: create a space where students can learn, build and experiment with technologies that form the future of the digital world.

🥽

What is the Metaverse?

A vast digital world that combines Virtual Reality and Augmented Reality, letting people live and interact inside a three-dimensional environment that resembles real life.

🥽

Virtual Reality (VR)

You enter a fully virtual world with a headset like Oculus or HTC Vive, completely separated from the real one.

Examples: Half-Life: Alyx, Beat Saber, Ready Player One.

📱

Augmented Reality (AR)

Digital elements (3D images, text, characters) are added on top of the real world, seen through a phone camera or special glasses.

Examples: Pokémon GO, Snapchat filters, Iron Man’s helmet HUD.

🌐

Where they meet

The metaverse uses VR to enter a virtual world and AR to blend it with real life. People can play, study, work and communicate using both.
